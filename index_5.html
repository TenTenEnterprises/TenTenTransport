Services

Every mode. One standard.

However your freight needs to move, it gets the same treatment: an accountable owner, carriers vetted through Armstrong's compliance program, and proactive communication from pickup through delivery.

Full Truckload

Dependable 53' dry van capacity across the U.S. and cross-border into Canada and Mexico. Spot coverage, dedicated lanes, and surge capacity for when your routing guide falls short.

LTL

Enterprise-grade LTL rates through Armstrong's national carrier programs — with a real person to call when a shipment needs attention, not a case number.

Flatbed & Open Deck

Steel coils, concrete block, machinery, and building products — moved by open-deck carriers with the right securement gear and the experience to use it properly.

Refrigerated

Food-grade reefer capacity for cold-chain shippers who can't miss appointments — from single-temp truckload to fully managed multi-stop, multi-temp distribution programs.

Rail & Intermodal

Nationwide rail service — coast to coast and Mexico to Canada, refrigerated and dry. Rail economics on your long lanes, with one point of contact instead of a railroad phone tree.

Specialized Programs & Hazmat

Hazmat-certified capacity, project freight, and managed equipment: we operate specialized trailers and dedicated fleets on our clients' behalf to run complex multi-stop, multi-temp operations.

Contract, RFP & Dedicated Pricing

Pricing you can build a budget on. We bid quarterly and annual lanes to hold — not to win now and renegotiate later — and we stand behind every number through the life of the award.

How every load works

  • Quote. Priced by a person who knows the lane and the market — not an algorithm's best guess.
  • Cover. Every carrier passes Armstrong's vetting and compliance screen before a wheel turns.
  • Track. Proactive updates from your rep and the ops team behind them. If something goes sideways, you hear it from us first — with a fix attached.
  • Deliver & support. Clean PODs, responsive billing through Armstrong's back office, and answers that don't stop when the load delivers.

How You're Supported

One point of contact. A whole team behind them.

You'll always know exactly who to call — and you'll never be stuck waiting on one person. Every TenTen account is layered four deep.

Layer 01

Your Dedicated Rep

One person who knows your freight, your facilities, and your priorities — and owns your business end to end. No ticket queues, no starting over with a stranger.

Layer 02

The TenTen Ops Team

A full operations team behind your rep, tracking loads and clearing problems around the clock. If your rep is on a plane, your freight never notices.

Layer 03

The Armstrong Network

Armstrong Transport Group's national infrastructure — carrier vetting and compliance, claims support, and a professional billing back office on every load.

Layer 04

Technology & Fraud Defense

AI-assisted carrier screening and identity verification layered on top of human vetting. In an era of double-brokering and freight fraud, every carrier is checked by software and by people before your freight moves.

Why shippers stay with TenTen

  • Cradle-to-grave accountability. The person who quotes your freight sees it through to delivery.
  • Freight doesn't sleep — neither do we. Real answers on nights and weekends, when things actually go sideways.
  • Long-term over transactional. We'd rather earn your freight for the next ten years than squeeze the next ten loads.
  • Modern tools, human judgment. We put current technology and AI to work on tracking, screening, and fraud prevention — and keep people in charge of every decision.
