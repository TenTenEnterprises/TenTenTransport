What we offer

  • Base salary plus tiered commission. Your split climbs from 25% up to 38% as production grows — and the plan doesn't move once you're on it.
  • Remote flexibility. Work from where you work best, with a preference for producers around Chattanooga, Nashville, Knoxville, or Atlanta so the team can stay connected.
